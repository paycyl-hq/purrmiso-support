Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 28, 2026

PurrMiso: Kitten Merge & Cats is built with a strict privacy-first philosophy. Core sanctuary and merge gameplay operates 100% offline. We do not require account registration, login credentials, or personal contact details (such as your name, email address, phone number, physical address, or contacts).

All of your gameplay progress—including your bred cats, Mendelian genetic traits, Pedigree Dex collection, Merge-2 board items, Gold Fish and Gem balances, and audio, language, or accessibility preferences—is stored strictly locally on your device. We do not operate external gameplay servers to store or profile your in-game activity.

Third-Party Services & Optional Rewarded Advertising

To keep PurrMiso free to play without forced pop-up interruptions, the game integrates optional, player-initiated rewarded video ads powered by Google AdMob:

  • Google AdMob: When you voluntarily choose to watch an opt-in rewarded video ad (for example, to refill energy, speed up breeding incubation, double offline fish rewards, or boost rare trait odds), Google AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Advertising Identifier / IDFA, strictly when authorized), product interaction events (ad impressions and taps), and coarse diagnostic metrics to serve ads, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
  • App Tracking Transparency (ATT) & Consent (GDPR / UMP): On iOS, PurrMiso requests your permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework and, in applicable regions (such as the EEA and UK), presents Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form before serving personalized advertisements. If you decline tracking or personalized consent, full gameplay remains available and any optional rewarded ads shown are non-personalized.

Data Retention & Your Controls

Because your save data resides locally on your device, uninstalling PurrMiso: Kitten Merge & Cats permanently removes all local game progress and settings. You can modify or revoke advertising tracking permissions at any time under iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app updates or legal requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
